# Go Bjoun logo concept

The mark evolves the original flight-path idea into a more distinctive system:

- The route begins as a subtle lowercase `g`.
- Its open counter creates useful negative space at small sizes.
- The rising stroke communicates movement and becomes the aircraft.
- The complete identity remains one-colour so it works on photography, print,
  merchandise, social avatars, and favicons.

## Files

- `go-bjoun-logo.svg`: primary horizontal lockup.
- `go-bjoun-mark.svg`: standalone avatar and favicon mark.
- `go-bjoun-logo-concept.png`: generated exploration board and visual reference.

## Usage

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the wordmark's `o`.
Use the near-black primary colour `#111916` on light backgrounds. On dark
backgrounds, the SVG paths and text can be changed to warm ivory `#FFF9EF`.

Do not stretch the logo, add shadows, place it inside a circle, or separate the
aircraft from its route line.
